Tickets

Three tickets per A4 sheet, cut crosswise into 210 × 99 mm. Each has a tear-off stub with its number.

Details

Tickets

One guest per line: name, then table and seat if you have them. Separate them with commas, or paste straight from a spreadsheet. With a list, you get one ticket per guest.

Look

  1. In the print dialog choose A4, margins None, scale 100% (not "Fit"), and turn on Background graphics.
  2. Use thick white card (200–300 gsm) if your printer takes it. Not blue card: printer ink is see-through, so gold and white won't show on it.
  3. Navy covers almost the whole sheet in ink. For more than a few sheets, a print shop is usually cheaper, or choose Ivory.
  4. Cut at the small grey ticks, 99 mm and 198 mm from the top. A guillotine or craft knife and ruler gives the cleanest edge.
  5. Optional: score the dashed line before the stub so it tears off neatly.
