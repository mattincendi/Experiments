Tickets

Three tickets per A4 sheet, cut crosswise. Each has a tear-off stub with its number.

Details

Tickets

One ticket per line: name, table, seat, separated by commas, or paste three columns straight from a spreadsheet. Leave the name out for a seat-only ticket (, 4, 2). The table and seat print on the ticket and the stub.

Numbers tickets without a table and seat automatically: with 10, tickets 1–10 are table 1, seats 1–10, then table 2, and so on.

QR code

The QR code on the stub opens this link. Saved in this browser only. Check that the link opens on a phone that isn't signed in to your work account. Leave it empty for a barcode of the ticket number instead.

Look

Cutting

  1. In the print dialog choose A4, margins None, scale 100% (not "Fit"), and turn on Background graphics.
  2. Use thick white card (200–300 gsm) if your printer takes it. Not blue card: printer ink is see-through, so gold and white won't show on it.
  3. Navy covers almost the whole sheet in ink. For more than a few sheets, a print shop is usually cheaper, or choose Ivory.
  4. Cut along the black trim marks at the edges of the sheet: line each mark up with the blade of your cutting board. Do the two long sides first, then the four cuts across.
  5. Optional: score the dashed line before the stub so it tears off neatly.
